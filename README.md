# Pregenesis Saga

> **An open-source documentary-inspired webtoon exploring deep time, interconnectedness, hidden possibilities, and the pursuit of human flourishing.**

---

## Overview

**Pregenesis Saga** is an open-source creative and research project that combines visual storytelling, documentary narration, philosophy, science, ecology, history, theology, and speculative fiction into a unified webtoon experience.

Rather than focusing solely on individual heroes or singular events, the series explores the often-unseen networks of relationships, generations, ecosystems, cultures, and ideas that quietly shape civilizations and the future.

At its heart is a simple question:

> **What invisible forces make flourishing possible long before anyone notices them?**

---

## Vision

The project seeks to tell stories that reveal:

* the interconnectedness of life
* the significance of forgotten people and overlooked contributions
* the emergence of civilization across deep time
* resilience through adaptation
* the relationship between nature, culture, and technology
* hope rooted in cooperation rather than domination

Every episode examines how today's realities emerge from countless visible and invisible contributions inherited across generations.

## Storytelling Style

Pregenesis Saga blends multiple storytelling traditions:

* Documentary narration
* Cinematic webtoon panels
* Graphic essays
* Visual metaphors
* Historical reconstruction
* Scientific explanation
* Philosophical reflection
* Character-driven narratives

The result is intended to feel somewhere between a documentary film, an illustrated essay, and an epic webtoon.
